
## Mountains and mines

1. Reduce number of cities to 5
2. On top half map deplay 4 hill hexes. Cant be first row, first column, last column. Building over hill is -3 cost. Hill cant be on city, river, lake, factory.
3. Deploy 2 mines. Scan map for hills, look at hex near hill without other features. Deploy mine here. Move to next hill. If no empty near hill move again. If run out hills, well, we have less mines. Mines has icon like top half circle. Could be with empty inside. Like tunel. 
4. Deploy 2 forge. Scan map from bottom to look for cities. Check for empty tile near city. Deploy forge. Move to next city. Icon: trapezoid, but long top. Like anvil. Maybe some timeout. Not the next city. But the city after next. 
5. Forge/Mine works like Forest/Sawmill just different pair. *10 for each pair of forge mine in our network.
6. Deploy Vineyard. Scan map from  bottom, but along the river tiles. One nearby tile needs to be river. No other feature. Place 1 vineyard. Then jump 2 row ups. Scan again. Deploy second vineyard. Connecting Vineyard gives +5 points but is optional. Dont need to finish map.
